# data-raw

`make_data.R` builds the datasets in `data/` from the CSVs in this folder.

## Provenance of the CSVs

| File | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `ab_si_ecosite.csv` | Bjelanovic & Comeau 2019, *MGM Research Note #2019-1*, Appendix 1 (breast height age 50) and Appendix 3 (total age 50). |
| `ab_si_edatope.csv` | Bjelanovic & Comeau 2019, *MGM Research Note #2019-1*, Appendix 2 (breast height age 50) and Appendix 4 (total age 50). |
| `si_ecosite_guides.csv` | Comeau 2020, *MGM Research Note #2020-1*, Tables 1-6. |

The CSVs were generated by extracting each PDF word together with its page
coordinates and assigning it to a column by x-position, rather than by parsing
the flattened text stream. The published tables are sparse (most species
columns are blank for any given ecosite), and a plain text extraction collapses
the blanks, which silently shifts values into the wrong species. Every numeric
cell was checked to fall inside a declared column band, and the per-page cell
counts were reconciled against the printed tables.

Coefficient tables (`ab_si_conversions`, `ab_gypsy_coefs`) are transcribed
directly in `make_data.R` from:

- Bjelanovic & Comeau 2019, *MGM Research Note #2019-2*, Tables 1 and 1a.
- Huang, Meng & Yang 2009, *GYPSY* Tech. Rep. T/216, Table 1 and Appendix 1.

## Known quirks reproduced verbatim

- `ab_si_ecosite` / `ab_si_edatope`: in Appendix 2 and Appendix 4 the SASK 6C
  trembling aspen row carries the same mean and standard deviation (17.47,
  2.41) on both the breast height and total age basis. This is how it is
  printed; it is almost certainly a typographical error in the source, but it
  has not been altered here.
- `si_ecosite_guides`: soil moisture and nutrient regime codes are kept as the
  published range strings (for example `4-6`, `b-d`, `md-vm`). The coding
  differs between guides; see `?si_ecosite_guides`.
